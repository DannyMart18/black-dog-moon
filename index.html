About

Black Dog Moon

Five-piece blues-infused rock 'n' roll out of County Down, Northern Ireland. Formed in 2022, Black Dog Moon have built a reputation as one of the most electrifying live acts on the Irish rock circuit.

Their self-titled debut album arrived in June 2024 — a statement of intent from a band who wear their influences loudly and play them with conviction. Follow-up single Neon Queen sharpened the edges in September 2025, setting up sophomore album Hell and Back, released November 28, 2025 and road-tested at Planet Rockstock.

Hell and Back earned a glowing 8/10 from Powerplay Rock & Metal in their March/April 2026 edition — cementing Black Dog Moon as a band very much in the ascendant.
